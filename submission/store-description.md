# Harbor - Payout Funding Audit

Are net cash payouts covered by the business?

Harbor follows reported operating cash through fixed-asset purchases, dividends and net repurchases. An on-chart waterfall, exact numerical receipt and TTM comparison table show what remains after those uses of cash. Payout coverage answers a specific question: how much cash after fixed investment was generated for each unit of net cash paid to shareholders?

Change the **OCF stress drop %** input to test a 0–50% decline in operating cash while holding investment and payouts fixed. Harbor shows the resulting funding remainder and prominent operating-cash headroom before a gap appears (or the rise needed to close an existing gap). This is a sensitivity test, not a forecast.

Cash reserves, total debt, net borrowing or repayment, and year-over-year share-count change provide context. Their presence does not prove that a particular financing source funded a particular payout.

**How to use it:** inspect the reported coverage and remainder, compare recent TTM periods, then test a cash decline before researching an income or capital-return idea. A shortfall is a reason to inspect the statements, not a prediction of a dividend cut or a buy/sell instruction.

**Definitions:** FCF here is operating cash minus fixed-asset purchases; provider FCF is cross-checked when available. Net repurchases offset cash share issuance, so they are not gross buybacks. Net cash payout is dividends plus positive net repurchases. The remainder excludes acquisitions, other investing flows and debt principal; it is not the change in cash. Amounts are scaled in the provider's reporting currency.

**Data discipline:** four consecutive fiscal quarters are aligned by quarter and exact period end. Missing is never zero. If equity cash flow is missing, the dividend audit and dividend condition remain available when their own inputs are complete, while net payout stays unknown. Other missing core inputs are labeled LIMITED DATA. Duplicate records invalidate only the affected field and period. Unsuitable sectors and stale statements suppress current conditions. Banks, insurers and real estate require other payout measures and are outside this model.

**Time boundary:** this is a latest-reported snapshot. Fiscal period ends are not filing-availability timestamps, and history can be revised. TTM comparisons use the current dataset. The three registered current-state conditions populate only the latest bar; Harbor does not provide historical trading signals or point-in-time backtests.
