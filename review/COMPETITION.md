# Public entry review · October 24, 2026

The [official collection](https://trendspider.com/trading-tools-store/collection/hackathon26/?post_type=indicator) and [Fundamentals category](https://trendspider.com/trading-tools-store/indicators/category/fundamentals/) were checked through public search results and listing descriptions. Public entries are visible; unpublished projects are not. This is a focused review, not a complete census. A collection listing does not itself prove the category an entrant selected.

| Reviewed concept | Public emphasis | Harbor's distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Valuation Bands](https://trendspider.com/trading-tools-store/indicators/6ab181-valuation-bands/) | Historical valuation multiples converted into price bands | Harbor does not estimate a stock price or fair multiple |
| [Valuation Floors & Ceilings](https://trendspider.com/trading-tools-store/indicators/6aac45-valuation-floors-ceilings/) | Price levels derived from low/high historical multiples | Harbor reconciles cash actually generated and paid out |
| Fundamental Inflection Radar, visible in the collection | Growth, margin, cash-flow, efficiency and balance-sheet composites, plus their changes | Harbor has no weighted composite; every headline follows a cash identity |
| Winvest Fundamental Rating, visible in Fundamentals | Earnings grade and buy zone | Harbor gives a funding remainder and explicit sensitivity, without an entry or stop |
| Fundamentals Value Gap, visible in Fundamentals | Valuation relative to fundamental data | Harbor tests capital-allocation funding, independent of market price |
| What's Priced In, visible in the Store | Peer premium and implied growth | Harbor uses reported cash flows, not reverse valuation assumptions |

The differentiating workflow is: **reported cash receipt → payout gap → financing context → fixed-assumption stress**. Conventional cash-flow arithmetic is not novel mathematics. We have not copied competitor code or artwork. Descriptions are the authors' claims and were not independently validated.

The strongest demonstrable feature is not a badge: on the captured CVX data, 1.14× payout coverage coexists with only 7.2% operating-cash headroom, while a 20% decline produces a funding gap. On CSCO, the baseline itself shows a shortfall. These are arithmetic examples, not return tests or forecasts.
